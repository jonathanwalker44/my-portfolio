About me

Hands-on IT work in a large public-sector environment, backed by a degree path in information technology.

I'm a senior Information Technology student at William Paterson University and an IT Specialist for the State of New Jersey. My day-to-day work is helping people and systems stay productive: supporting users, troubleshooting issues, and working with the tools that large organizations depend on.

I'm most interested in how enterprise environments are built and protected. That includes identity and access with Active Directory and Entra ID, device management with Intune, Microsoft 365 administration, and the networking and security practices that tie it all together.
